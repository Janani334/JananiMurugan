\# TrendKart Fashion Enterprise – Sales \& Business Analytics





\## 1. Business Understanding



TrendKart Fashion Enterprise is a retail business dealing with multiple product categories, brands, stores, regions and sales channels. The business generates transaction-level data covering sales, customers, products, stores, payments, discounts, returns and profitability.



Analyzing this data helps management understand business performance, identify high-performing areas and discover opportunities for revenue and profit improvement.



\## 2. Project Objective



The objective of this project is to analyze TrendKart Fashion Enterprise's sales data to evaluate \*\*sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance and regional trends\*\*.



The project aims to develop meaningful KPIs and an interactive Excel dashboard that provide actionable insights and support data-driven business decisions.



\## 3. Business Questions



\- Which product categories generate the highest sales?

\- Which regions and stores perform better?

\- How do sales and profit change over time?

\- Which brands contribute the most to sales?

\- Which sales channels generate more revenue?

\- Which payment methods are most preferred?

\- How do membership segments contribute to sales?

\- What is the regional return performance?

\- Who are the highest-value customers?

\- How are stores performing against their targets?

\## 5. Data Profiling



Data profiling was performed before analysis to understand the \*\*structure, completeness, consistency and quality\*\* of the datasets.



The profiling process examined duplicate records, missing values, inconsistent formats, incorrect spellings, invalid values, formatting issues and business-rule violations.



A separate \*\*Data Quality Log\*\* was maintained to document the identified issues and their impact.



\## 6. Data Cleaning



The identified data-quality issues were reviewed and corrected before performing the final analysis.



The cleaning process included removing duplicate records, standardizing dates and categorical values, correcting spelling inconsistencies, handling missing identifiers, converting numeric fields into appropriate data types and validating business-related values.



| Data Area | Issue Identified | Action Taken |

|---|---|---|

| Sales | Mixed date formats | Dates standardized |

| Sales | Quantity stored as text | Converted to numeric |

| Sales | Payment mode inconsistencies | Standardized |

| Sales | Return status errors | Corrected |

| Customers | Duplicate phone numbers | Identified and reviewed |

| Customers | Invalid email addresses | Identified and corrected |

| Customers | Inconsistent membership values | Standardized |

| Products | Duplicate product names | Identified and reviewed |

| Products | Incorrect category spellings | Corrected |

| Stores | Store status errors | Standardized |



\## 7. Data Analysis



The cleaned data was analyzed using \*\*Excel PivotTables, PivotCharts, calculated metrics, aggregations and comparisons\*\*.



The analysis focused on sales, cost, profit, quantity, discounts, GST, products, brands, regions, stores, customers, memberships, payment modes, sales channels and returns.



These analyses were used to identify trends, high-performing segments, customer behavior and potential areas for improvement.

\## 11. Key Insights



The dashboard analysis produced the following key business findings.



| Area | Key Insight |

|---|---|

| Products | \*\*Women Sarees\*\* recorded the highest category-level sales at approximately \*\*₹11.03 lakh\*\*. |

| Regions | \*\*South Zone 1\*\* recorded the highest sales at approximately \*\*₹44.63 lakh\*\*. |

| Sales Channel | \*\*Offline sales\*\* contributed approximately \*\*₹60.49 lakh\*\*, higher than Online sales of approximately \*\*₹31.78 lakh\*\*. |

| Payment Mode | \*\*UPI\*\* recorded the highest sales contribution at approximately \*\*₹26.19 lakh\*\*. |

| Brands | \*\*Grt Jewellers\*\* was the top-performing brand with approximately \*\*₹5.51 lakh\*\* in sales. |

| Membership | \*\*Regular members\*\* generated the highest membership sales contribution at approximately \*\*₹29.74 lakh\*\*. |

| Returns | Returned transactions represented approximately \*\*4.87%\*\* of total transactions. |

| Monthly Trend | \*\*October 2024\*\* recorded the highest monthly sales, while January 2025 recorded the lowest. |



\## 12. Recommendations



Based on the dashboard findings, the following recommendations can support business improvement.



| Business Area | Recommendation |

|---|---|

| Products | Increase inventory availability for consistently high-performing categories and brands. |

| Regions | Study successful practices from high-performing regions and apply them to lower-performing areas. |

| Customers | Strengthen loyalty programs and targeted offers for valuable membership segments. |

| Payments | Continue promoting convenient digital payment methods, particularly UPI. |

| Returns | Analyze major return causes and take corrective actions to reduce return rates. |

| Sales | Use monthly sales trends to plan promotions and inventory more effectively. |



\## 13. Project Outcome



The project transformed raw retail transaction data into a structured analytical solution covering \*\*sales, profitability, products, brands, customers, regions, stores, channels, payments and returns\*\*.



The final interactive dashboard converts the analysis into clear business insights that can support \*\*sales planning, customer engagement, inventory management and data-driven decision-making\*\*.
