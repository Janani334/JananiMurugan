\# FreshMart Business Performance Analysis





\## 1. Project Overview



FreshMart Business Performance Analysis is an Excel-based data analytics project developed to evaluate sales performance, revenue, profitability, product categories, regional performance, customer membership, and sales channels.



The project uses Microsoft Excel for data profiling, data cleaning, exploratory analysis, KPI development, Pivot Table analysis, and dashboard creation to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights.



\## 2. Business Understanding



FreshMart operates across different product categories, regions, store types, customer membership levels, and sales channels.



The business generates transaction-level data containing information related to customers, products, stores, sales, discounts, revenue, profit, membership, sales channels, and transaction dates.



Analyzing this information helps understand overall business performance, identify high-performing areas, and recognize opportunities for improvement.



\## 3. Business Questions



The analysis was designed to answer the following key business questions:



1\. What is the monthly revenue and profit?

2\. What is product category performance?

3\. What are the regional sales?

4\. What is regional membership revenue?

5\. What is revenue by channel and day type?



\## 4. Dataset Overview



The project uses multiple datasets related to FreshMart's business operations.



| Dataset | Purpose |

|---|---|

| Sales Transactions | Contains transaction-level sales, quantity, discount, revenue, cost, profit, payment mode, sales channel, and day-type information |

| Customers | Contains customer and membership information |

| Products | Contains product, category, subcategory, brand, cost, selling price, and season information |

| Stores | Contains store, region, store type, and monthly target information |

| Employees | Contains employee and department information |

| Suppliers | Contains supplier-related information |



The cleaned Sales Transactions dataset contains 2,500 transaction records with 33 fields after combining the required supporting information.



\## 5. Data Profiling



Data profiling was performed to understand the structure, completeness, consistency, and quality of the available datasets.



The data was reviewed for missing or blank values, unknown entries, inconsistent categorical values, duplicate records, and other data-quality issues before analysis.



\## 6. Data Cleaning



The identified data-quality issues were reviewed and corrected before performing the analysis.



The cleaning process included handling blank and unknown values, standardizing inconsistent entries, checking duplicate records, and organizing the datasets for analysis.



The cleaned data was validated to ensure consistency and reliability for the final analysis.



\## 7. Data Analysis



Exploratory data analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel to understand FreshMart's business performance and identify important trends.



The analysis focused on:



\- Monthly Revenue and Profit Trend

\- Product Category Performance

\- Sales Performance by Region

\- Regional Membership Revenue

\- Revenue Performance by Sales Channel \& Day Type



Pivot Tables, PivotCharts, Excel calculations, filters, and comparisons were used to summarize the data and identify meaningful business patterns.



\## 8. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)



Four key performance indicators were developed to provide a quick overview of FreshMart's overall business performance.



| KPI | Value |

|---|---:|

| Total Revenue | ₹2,868,432.60 |

| Total Profit | ₹737,358.89 |

| Profit Margin | 25.71% |

| Average Order Value | ₹1,151.98 |



\## 9. Dashboard Development



An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to provide a consolidated view of FreshMart's business performance.



The dashboard brings together the major KPIs and visual analysis covering monthly revenue and profit, product category performance, regional sales, regional membership revenue, and sales channel performance by day type.

\## 10. Key Insights



\- October recorded the highest monthly revenue and profit.

\- Beverages generated the highest revenue, while Bakery generated the highest profit.

\- South region recorded the highest sales contribution.

\- Gold members generated the highest membership revenue.

\- In-Store generated the highest overall sales revenue.

\- Online sales were slightly higher on weekends.



\## 11. Recommendations



\- Focus on high-performing products and improve low-performing categories.

\- Strengthen sales strategies in lower-performing regions.

\- Retain and engage high-value Gold members.

\- Increase Online and Mobile App promotions.

\- Monitor monthly sales and profit trends for better planning.
